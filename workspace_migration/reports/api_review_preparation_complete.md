**Date:** March 29, 2025

## Executive Summary

This report documents the successful completion of all preparation work for the API Review phase of the Navius Workspace Migration project. Over the past two weeks, we have developed comprehensive documentation, tools, templates, and processes to support a thorough and systematic review of all public APIs across the Navius framework. The API Review is scheduled to begin on April 1, 2025, and will span 10 weeks, concluding on June 10, 2025.
